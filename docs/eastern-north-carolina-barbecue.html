The whole hog

East of Raleigh, barbecue means the whole hog, cooked low and slow, chopped (at some pits with bits of crisp skin mixed back in) and dressed with vinegar, salt and red and black pepper. There's no tomato in the sauce. Coleslaw and cornbread or hushpuppies come alongside. Skylight Inn BBQ in Ayden is a James Beard America's Classic.

The list at a glance

Towns with the most: Greenville (4), Raleigh (2), Ayden (1), Knightdale (1), Benson (1).

No current source, or a website gone dark, and it stayed off. Review sites played no part, and places run by region and town rather than by anybody's score.
